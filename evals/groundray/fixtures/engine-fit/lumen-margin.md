# Lumen October contribution margin

## Definition

For Mosaic Studio Lumen in October 2026, contribution margin is recognized fees
minus posted direct production costs, divided by recognized fees. Sales tax and
unposted owner-time estimates are excluded by the supplied fictional rule.[^owner]
The corrected ledger gives USD 4,000 fees and USD 1,500 costs:
(4,000 - 1,500) / 4,000 = **62.5%**.[^ledger][^correction]
